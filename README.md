## 📖 Vision

In the AI era, computational power and proprietary algorithms are creating a new digital divide. **AltAI** (Alternative AI) is a community-driven collection dedicated to **AI equalization**.

Our mission is to identify, curate, and promote open-source AI projects that serve as **genuine alternatives** to mainstream commercial software. We are not here to replace anything — we are here to add **one more choice** for every user.

> *No replacement. Just an alternative.*

### 🎯 What is the "New Format World"?

It is a state of balance where users have **real options**:

- You can use **ChatGPT Plus**, **and** you can also use **OpenAssistant** or **Vicuna** (run locally)
- You can use **Midjourney**, **and** you can also use **Stable Diffusion** + **ComfyUI**
- You can use **GitHub Copilot**, **and** you can also use **CodeLlama** + **Continue**
- You can use **ElevenLabs**, **and** you can also use **Piper** or **Bark**

More choices mean more freedom — freedom of privacy, freedom of cost, freedom of customization. We believe that in the software world, **equal rights come from alternatives, not monopolies**.

### 🤝 How to Contribute

We need you to build a world with more choices. Here's how:

1. **Add projects** – Submit a PR to include missing alternatives.
2. **Rank parity** – Help us score how close an open project is to its commercial counterpart.
3. **Write code** – Join sub-projects that need help (check issues).
4. **Spread the word** – Tell others that an alternative exists.

### 📜 Principles

- **No replacement, just alternatives** – We coexist, not compete.
- **Privacy first** – Your data stays yours whenever possible.
- **Open weights** – Models should be downloadable, not just API-access-only.
- **Community governance** – No corporate backdoors.
